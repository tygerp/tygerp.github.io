DEEP PACKET INSPECTION
FIELD MANUAL / QUICK START

Aim for precision.

You have three 45-second waves. Protect network integrity and deliver at least 70% of legitimate traffic to win.

  1. Inspect. Click Inspect on a packet to see its source, port, protocol, and purpose.
  2. Decide. Source IP identifies a sender. Destination port identifies a service. Protocol identifies the transport.
  3. Deploy. Build a block rule. Conditions inside a rule use AND; separate rules use OR.
  4. Refine. Watch the delivery rate. Delete overly broad rules using their × button. Rules persist between waves.

Scoring: +20 for a blocked threat, +5 for a delivered legitimate packet, −25 for a blocked legitimate packet, −30 and −6 integrity for a leaked threat. Integrity cannot recover during a mission.

Pause whenever you need thinking time. Packet labels are teaching aids; real firewalls do not automatically know whether traffic is malicious. This simplified simulator creates no real network packets.
