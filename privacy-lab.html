Your 30-second briefing

Legal and ethical are different questions.

Ethics · Standard 5.3.4

What should a developer do?

Ethics asks whether a choice respects people and avoids harm. A useful app should explain its data use and collect only what it needs. More data might help a business, but also put users' privacy at risk.

For example, a weather app could let you enter a city instead of storing every place you visit. These principles apply to adults and teens too. [1]

Law · Standard 5.3.5

What must a covered service do?

United States federal law: COPPA and its FTC Rule protect children under 13. They cover commercial online services directed to those children and other services that actually know they collect children's personal information.

Covered services generally need parental notice and verifiable parental consent before collection, with limited exceptions. Location detailed enough to identify a street and city counts as personal information. A child's tap on “Allow” is not the same as that consent. [2]

COPPA is not a blanket ban on location apps and does not cover all teenagers. Other laws may apply. An ethical concern alone does not prove something is illegal.

Behind the project: how this teaches the standards

By Tyger Phan, with AI assistance. This website focuses on location privacy. It asks visitors to apply ideas instead of only reading definitions.

5.3.4: Visitors balance useful features against unnecessary collection and privacy risks. 5.3.5: Visitors apply COPPA's under-13 scope and parental-consent rule to a fictional commercial app.

One code interaction: The situations array stores each question, its options, and the explanation for each option. Clicking an answer calls choose(index). It compares the chosen index with correct, updates the score, disables the choices to prevent repeat scoring, and reveals feedback. The Next button advances to the next situation.

What the code does: HTML organizes the page, CSS controls the appearance and mobile layout, and JavaScript runs the challenge. This page's code does not request GPS, send answers, use analytics, or save scores. Refreshing resets the game. The hosting provider can still receive normal web request data.
